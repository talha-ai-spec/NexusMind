# Image-to-Image / Photo Editing Setup & Usage Guide

This guide explains how to use AI Photo Editing and Image Restyling in the copilot application.

---

## 1. How to Edit a Photo in Chat

No complicated settings or external tools are required. You can edit any photo directly within your normal chat conversation:

1. Click the **`+`** icon on the bottom left of the chat composer.
2. Click **Add photos** (or drag and drop any image into the composer).
3. The photo appears as a thumbnail chip above the input box.
4. Type your creative editing instruction in plain English.
5. Press **Enter** or click the send button.
6. The assistant transforms the image in the cloud and displays the resulting masterpiece with a one-click **Download** button.

---

## 2. Recommended Prompt Styles

Pollinations.ai and FLUX image-to-image respond exceptionally well to stylistic and artistic descriptors:

### Anime & Cartoon
* *"turn this into anime style with vibrant colors and expressive lines"*
* *"convert this photo into a 3D Pixar animated character with warm lighting"*
* *"make this look like a Studio Ghibli watercolor scene"*

### Fine Art & Paintings
* *"turn this into an oil painting with thick impasto brush strokes"*
* *"make this a dramatic Baroque painting with chiaroscuro lighting by Rembrandt"*
* *"restyle this as a colorful Impressionist masterpiece like Claude Monet"*
* *"transform this into a minimalist Japanese ink wash painting (sumi-e)"*

### Sci-Fi, Cyberpunk & Fantasy
* *"restyle this photo into a neon cyberpunk scene with rain reflections"*
* *"turn this into a dark fantasy illustration with glowing magical runes"*
* *"make this look like an epic sci-fi concept art scene"*

### Vintage & Photography Styles
* *"make this look like a 1970s vintage Polaroid photograph with faded grain"*
* *"convert this into a moody black and white film photograph by Ansel Adams"*
* *"turn this into a detailed pencil sketch on parchment paper"*

---

## 3. Regenerating & Trying New Variations

* **Regenerate Button**: If you want another take on the edit, simply click the **Regenerate** button below the assistant message. The app will preserve the source photo and re-run inference with a new random seed.
* **Refining Prompts**: You can attach the same photo again and try different modifiers (e.g. adding *"cinematic lighting"*, *"octane render"*, or *"pastel palette"*).
